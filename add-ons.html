Build exactly what your business needs.

The $500 base site covers a professional 5-section website. These are the extras — what each one does, why it might be worth it for your business, and what changes the price.

Additional Page

$75 / page

The base site includes up to 5 sections (Home, About, Services, Gallery, Contact). If your business needs more — an FAQ page, a Team page, a Menu, Testimonials, or a dedicated page for a specific service — each one is added individually.

Worth it if: you have a specific topic customers ask about often enough that it deserves its own page instead of being squeezed into an existing section.

Logo Design or Refresh

$150–$300

A designed logo for businesses that don't have one yet, or a cleanup/modernization of an existing logo that looks outdated or doesn't work well on a website.

What changes the price: a full logo built from scratch (with a few concepts to choose from) runs toward the higher end; refreshing or vectorizing an existing logo you already like the core idea of is closer to $150.

Worth it if: your current logo is a low-resolution image, was made for a business card and not a website, or you don't have one at all.

Photo Gallery / Portfolio

$75

A dedicated section or page showing off real photos of your work — finished projects, products, your space, or your team in action.

Worth it if: your work is visual — contractors, salons, restaurants, artists, and anyone whose past work is the best sales pitch they have.

Appointment / Booking Scheduler

$150–$250

Lets customers book a time directly on your website instead of calling to check availability back and forth.

What changes the price: a simple embedded booking widget (connected to a calendar you already use) is toward the lower end; a more custom setup — multiple service types, staff-specific availability, automatic reminders — runs higher.

Worth it if: you're currently playing phone tag to schedule appointments, or a lot of your business depends on booked time slots (salons, consultations, services with limited daily capacity).

Online Store

$300+

A real shopping cart and checkout so customers can buy directly from your site — not just a page that lists products with a phone number to call.

What changes the price: $300 covers a store with up to 20 products and standard checkout. More products, variants (sizes, colors), shipping-rate setup, or tax configuration for multiple states add to that.

Worth it if: people are already trying to buy from you online, or your product is easy to sell without someone needing to see or try it first.

Google Business Profile Setup & Optimization

$75–$100

Your free Google listing — the one that shows your hours, reviews, and location when someone searches your business name or "near me." Getting it set up correctly (or fixed, if it's outdated or unclaimed) makes a real difference in whether local customers find you.

What changes the price: claiming and optimizing an existing-but-neglected listing is toward the lower end; setting one up from scratch with photos, categories, and posts configured is closer to $100.

Worth it if: you want to show up when someone searches for what you do nearby — this is often the single highest-impact, lowest-cost thing a local business can do.

Monthly Hosting, Updates & Support

$25–$50/mo

An optional retainer instead of paying per-change. Covers hosting and a set number of included updates each month (swapping a photo, updating hours, adding a sentence) without a separate invoice every time.

What changes the price: how many included changes you want covered per month — occasional small tweaks land near $25/mo, more frequent updates near $50/mo.

Worth it if: your site's info changes often (seasonal hours, rotating specials, frequent photo updates) and you'd rather have it handled than request-and-pay each time.
