The New First Impression

Today's customers judge you online before they ever meet you

A generation raised on phones doesn't wander in hoping for the best — they search, they scroll, and they decide in seconds whether a business feels legitimate. An attractive, trustworthy website has become the modern equivalent of a clean storefront and a firm handshake: often the very first impression you make, and for a lot of customers, the only one that matters before they ever pick up the phone. Businesses that show up looking professional get the benefit of the doubt. The ones that don't — or don't show up at all — get passed over for a competitor who does, even when the work itself is just as good.
