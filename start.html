Totally optional — check anything that sounds useful and we'll confirm final pricing together. Tap the i next to any add-on for more detail.

$75/page

The base site includes up to 5 sections. If you need more — an FAQ page, a Team page, a Menu, Testimonials, or a dedicated page for a specific service — each one is added individually. Worth it if you have a topic customers ask about often enough that it deserves its own page.

$150–$300

A designed logo for businesses that don't have one yet, or a cleanup/modernization of an existing logo that looks outdated or doesn't work well on a website. Worth it if your current logo is low-resolution, made for a business card, or you don't have one at all.

$75

A dedicated section or page showing off real photos of your work — finished projects, products, your space, or your team in action. Worth it if your work is visual — contractors, salons, restaurants, artists, and anyone whose past work is the best sales pitch they have.

$150–$250

Lets customers book a time directly on your website instead of calling to check availability. Worth it if you're currently playing phone tag to schedule appointments, or a lot of your business depends on booked time slots.

$300+

A real shopping cart and checkout so customers can buy directly from your site. $300 covers up to 20 products and standard checkout. Worth it if people are already trying to buy from you online, or your product is easy to sell without someone needing to see or try it first.

$75–$100

Your free Google listing — the one that shows your hours, reviews, and location when someone searches your business name or "near me." Often the single highest-impact, lowest-cost thing a local business can do.

$25–$50/mo

An optional monthly retainer instead of paying per-change — covers hosting and a set number of included updates each month. Worth it if your site's info changes often and you'd rather have it handled than request-and-pay each time.
